SentryQuery verify

Paste a claim. It is checked against a corpus of indexed documents by a Researcher agent that retrieves the relevant passages and a Critic agent that rules on whether they support the claim. You get PASS or FAIL and the exact passages the Critic read, not a paragraph to interpret.

First check after an idle period takes up to a minute while the free instance wakes.

A claim it caught

A recorded run, not a live one. This is the answer to "what has it ever caught".

"Deere reported net sales and revenues of $45.7 billion in fiscal 2025, an increase over the prior year."

FAILthe Critic ruled against the claim

The claim that net sales and revenues were $45.7 billion in fiscal 2025 is supported, but the statement that this represents an "increase over the prior year" is unsupported. The sources indicate that net sales and revenues decreased in 2025 compared to 2024.

deere-10k-2025.pdf · page 46

SELECTED FINANCIAL DATA 2025 2024 2023 2022 2021 2020 2019 2018 2017 2016 Net sales and revenues $ 45,684 $ 51,716 $ 61,251 $ 52,577 $ 44,024 $ 35,540 ... Net sales 38,917 44,759 55,565 47,917 39,737 31,272 ...

The figure in the claim is right. $45,684 million does round to $45.7 billion. Only the direction is wrong, and the passage the Critic was given carries the prior year beside it. A model asked to summarise this filing could produce that sentence, and the verdict is what stops it.

Calling it from a program

The page is a demo. The point is the endpoint, which returns JSON a caller branches on: verdict is PASS or FAIL, reason_code separates a claim the Critic rejected from one no passage covered, and evidence is the exact chunks the ruling was made against.
