Check a claim against the documents.

A Researcher agent retrieves the passages that bear on your claim. A Critic agent rules on whether they support it. You get PASS or FAIL and the exact text it read, not a paragraph to interpret.

A claim it caught

A recorded run. This is the answer to "what has it ever caught".

Deere reported net sales and revenues of $45.7 billion in fiscal 2025, an increase over the prior year.

FAIL
the Critic ruled against the claim

The claim that net sales and revenues were $45.7 billion in fiscal 2025 is supported, but the statement that this represents an "increase over the prior year" is unsupported. The sources indicate that net sales and revenues decreased in 2025 compared to 2024.

deere-10k-2025.pdf · page 46

SELECTED FINANCIAL DATA 2025 2024 2023 2022 Net sales and revenues $ 45,684 $ 51,716 $ 61,251 $ 52,577 Net sales 38,917 44,759 55,565 47,917

The figure is right. $45,684 million does round to $45.7 billion. Only the direction is wrong, and the prior year sits in the same row of the same table the Critic was handed. A model summarising this filing could produce that sentence, and the verdict is what stops it.
